**Introduction**

 Hello, and thank you for developing our Pet DayCare Management System! I’m Pat Purrs, and I run a busy daycare that cares for both dogs and cats of all breeds, sizes, and types. Keeping track of each pet’s details—like their attendance, breed or species-specific traits, neutered status, and any special considerations—is crucial for ensuring a safe and organized environment.

We also need to maintain records of their owners and be able to quickly check which pets are attending on any given day. Ideally, the system should let us add new pets, remove pets, view all registered pets, check pets in or out for specific days, and calculate weekly fees, taking into account special requirements such as breed size for dogs, indoor/outdoor status for cats, or other care-related considerations.

A simple menu-driven interface will allow our staff to efficiently manage multiple pets while keeping all relevant information organized. This system will help us run the daycare smoothly, ensuring every pet has a happy and safe stay, while giving us the flexibility to track species-specific needs and requirements.
