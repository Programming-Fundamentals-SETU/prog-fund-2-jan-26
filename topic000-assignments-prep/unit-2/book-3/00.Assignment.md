# Assignment 2


Animal DayCare Management System - worth 60% of module grade



**Introduction**

 Here’s your updated spec with a **new business identity and richer backstory**, while keeping it clear and suitable for students:

---

Hello, and thank you for developing our Animal Daycare Management System!

I’m Anita Pet, owner of **Urban Tails Daycare**, a busy inner-city animal daycare designed especially for professionals working in the city. Many of our clients have demanding schedules, so they rely on us to provide a safe, caring, and stimulating environment for their pets during the day.

At Urban Tails, we currently care for **dogs, cats, and parrots**, each with their own unique needs and personalities. I have a particular passion for parrots as I previously bred and trained them for films and high-profile clients.  Ensuring their enrichment and wellbeing is especially important to me.

Keeping track of each animal’s details is essential. We need to store information such as:

    - Type of animal (dog, cat, or parrot)
    - Breed or species-specific traits
    - Age and general temperament
    - Neutered status (where applicable)
    - Any special care requirements (e.g. dog size, indoor/outdoor cats, parrot socialisation needs)

We also need to maintain accurate records of each animal’s owner and be able to quickly check which animals are attending on any given day.

The system should allow staff to:

    - Add new animals
    - Remove animals
    - View all registered animals
    - Check animals in and out for specific days of the week
    - Track attendance
    - Calculate weekly fees, taking into account different care needs (e.g. dog size, cat lifestyle, or additional enrichment for parrots)

A simple, menu-driven interface will allow our staff to efficiently manage multiple animals while keeping all relevant information organised.

This system will help us run Urban Tails Daycare smoothly, ensuring every animal—whether it barks, purrs, or talks—has a safe, happy, and enriching stay.
