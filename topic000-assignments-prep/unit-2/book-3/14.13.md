### Interview Process

**Not all students will be interviewed.**

- We reserve the right to ask a student to attend an online interview on your code. These interviews are to ensure that the code is written and understood by you (of course you can use other sources but they must be fully referenced and fully understood)

- Interviews to demonstrate the understanding of the submitted Assignment 2 code will take place in the week after Jan 11th. 

- We are very rigorous in checking that the work is yours and if we find that it is not yours or that you have plagarised, then we will follow the college procedures.

We will send out a slack message letting all students know when we are finished contacting all those that have been chosen for an interview.

### Interviews

It is compulsory that you are available to be interviewed on your submission in order to demonstrate both Understanding and Authorship. These interviews will take place between Monday Jan 12th to Friday 16th Jan.

Not all students will be interviewed. 


During your interview, you will be asked to explain your work. This is to ensure that the work is your own. You may be asked (for example) to explain:

- Any of the code.
- How inheritance is implemented, how interfaces are implemented
- How specific methods work.
- etc.

If you do not present for your interview you will receive 0 for the interview.  As the interview grade is a multiplier for your assigment grade, should you fail to show for your interview, you will receive zero, resulting in an overall grade of zero for your submission.

If you are not called for interview you will be given full marks for that portion. We will send out a slack message letting all students know when we are finished contacting all those that have been chosen for an interview.

 We understand your need for flexibility re times, dates  so we will publish a set of possible times nearer the time and you can pick a time that suits you. If you cannot be available during that week , please let us know well beforehand. We can facilitate an earlier interview (after you have submitted the assignment) if you are not available that week. 


### Referencing external materials

If you use code other than from lectures and labs (e.g. from StackOverFlow, other GitHub accounts, ai bots etc), you should always reference it. Failure to do so is plagiarism and will lead to a zero mark.



### How is the final mark calculated?

The final mark is calculated using the following formula:

- (((Functionality of Code ) ) * Interview Mark) / 10

Example:

- Functionality of code = 70/100
- Interview Mark = 9/10
- Final mark = ((70 + 6) * 9) / 10 = 68.4%
