# Using AI for Test Case Generation – Reflection Requirements

For this assignment, you may choose to:
- Write your **JUnit tests manually** using class notes, **or**
- Use an AI tool (e.g. ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.) to help generate some or all of your test cases


## Reflection Requirements (Mandatory)

You must include a **reflection section** in your submission that clearly explains **how your tests were created**.

---

## If You Used AI

You are required to document and critique your use of AI.

### 1. Prompts Used
Include:
- The **exact prompts** you entered into the AI tool  
- If you refined your prompts, include **each version**


### 2. Evaluation of AI Output

You must critically evaluate the generated tests. Consider:

- Were the tests **correct**?
- Did they cover **boundary values** properly?
- Were any tests **missing**?
- Did any tests **fail**? Why?
- Did the AI misunderstand your code?

You should identify at least:
- **2 strengths** of the AI-generated tests  
- **2 weaknesses / issues**


### 3. Improvements Made

Explain:
- What you **changed, fixed, or added**
- Why those changes were necessary

**Example:**
- “AI did not test invalid phone numbers, so I added those tests”
- “AI assumed validation in the constructor that did not exist”


### 4. Final Judgement

Briefly answer:
- Would you trust AI-generated tests in a real project?
- What are the risks of relying on AI for testing?

## If You Did NOT Use AI

### 1. Approach
- How you designed your tests (e.g. boundary value analysis, class notes, examples)

### 2. Challenges
- What you found difficult

### 3. Learning
- What you learned about testing

## Tip

AI can generate tests quickly—but it often:
- Misses **edge cases**
- Misinterprets **validation rules**
- Assumes behaviour that doesn’t exist

Your role is to act as the **developer reviewing the tests**, not just accepting them.
