### Interview Process

Interviews to demonstrate the understanding of the submitted Programing code will be written instead of verbal.

- This written exam will take place on Tuesday 5th May, location and time to be confirmed nearer the date. Date will be decided when the exam schedules are published. It is compulsory that you attend this written exam (i.e. it is a scheduled written exam like the other exams on your timetable).

- We will bring the paper for the exam. Please make sure you have pens, etc. with you.

- Laptops, phones, etc not permitted to be used during the exam. If you have been given permission to use a laptop for your other written exams, please notify **BOTH** Mairead and Siobhan in advance. If you have been given any other allowances for written exams, please let us know well in advance also.

- This is a [sample written exam](archives/SampleWrittenInterview.pdf) illustrating the types of questions that may be asked. Please familiarise yourself with the style of questions that can be asked.

- Grading for the assignment is based on the submitted project. The written in-class exam is only used to gauge the quality of the submitted code versus your level of understanding. It will not be used as a multiplier for the project, nor will it be graded.

- If the quality of the written interview exam differs significantly from the submitted project, we will ask you to come in for a compulsory verbal interview to explain your submitted code. This verbal interview grade will be used as a multiplier for your project grade.

- If the quality of the written interview exam is similar to the quality of the submitted project, there will be no need for a verbal interview and your full grade for the submitted code will be processed. We will send out an email letting all students know when we are finished contacting all those we wish to recall for a verbal interview.

- Please note that failure to show for the written exam (without documented mitigating circumstances) will result in you automatically being scheduled for a verbal interview. As stated above, all verbal interviews are used as multipliers for your project code.

### Interviews

Not all students will be interviewed. 


During your interview, you will be asked to explain your work. This is to ensure that the work is your own. You may be asked (for example) to explain:

- Any of the code.
- How inheritance is implemented, how interfaces are implemented
- How specific methods work.
- etc.

If you do not present for your interview you will receive 0 for the interview.  As the interview grade is a multiplier for your assigment grade, should you fail to show for your interview, you will receive zero, resulting in an overall grade of zero for your submission.

If you are not called for interview you will be given full marks for that portion. We will send out a slack message letting all students know when we are finished contacting all those that have been chosen for an interview.



### Referencing external materials

If you use code other than from lectures and labs (e.g. from StackOverFlow, other GitHub accounts, ai bots etc), you should always reference it. Failure to do so is plagiarism and will lead to a zero mark.



### How is the final mark calculated?

The final mark is calculated using the following formula:

- (((Functionality of Code ) ) * Interview Mark) / 10

Example:

- Functionality of code = 70/100
- Interview Mark = 9/10
- Final mark = ((70 + 6) * 9) / 10 = 68.4%
